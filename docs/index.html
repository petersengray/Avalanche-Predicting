West Shore Corn Clock

When the snow softens to corn, and when it gets too wet, on Lake Tahoe West Shore tours.
Frozen: still firm from the overnight refreeze. Corn: surface has softened and should ski well. Too wet: deep, sloppy snow and rising wet-slide danger — be off it.
